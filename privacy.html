Dandrey — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-16

Dandrey ("the app") is made by Itay Bickson, an individual developer in Israel, who is responsible for this policy. The app is designed to be private by default. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

The short version

  • Dandrey analyzes your photos and videos entirely on your device.
  • Dandrey never uploads or sends your photos, videos, or any information derived from them to us or any third party.
  • We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data.
  • There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking.

Photo library access

The app requests access to your photo library so it can:

  • scan photos and videos to find duplicates, near-duplicates, blurry, too-dark and overexposed shots, and large videos;
  • save new copies you ask for, such as a compressed version of a video or a still photo made from a Live Photo; and
  • move items you select to the system "Recently Deleted" album when you choose to clean up.

All analysis happens on your device, using Apple's on-device frameworks (such as Vision and Core ML) and a small image-recognition model built into the app. The app does not send your photos or anything about them off your device.

If you use iCloud Photos, Apple's Photos framework may download photos from your own iCloud account to your device: when you turn on the optional setting to analyze iCloud originals, and when you open a photo full screen. The images stay on your device. If iCloud Photos is on, new copies the app saves (such as a compressed video) sync to your iCloud like any other photo.

Deletion

When you choose to clean up, iOS asks you to confirm, and the selected items are moved to the system "Recently Deleted" album. They can be recovered there until you empty that album or iOS removes them. Dandrey cannot permanently delete your photos on its own.

Cleanup history

To help you recover from a cleanup, the app keeps a short history of what it moved to Recently Deleted: the date, how many items, their total size and up to four small thumbnails. This history is stored only on your iPhone (and in your device backups, if you make them), is never sent anywhere, and each entry is deleted automatically after 30 days. You can clear it at any time in Dandrey › Settings › Cleanup history.

Stored on your device

Scan results, your settings and the space-freed totals shown in the widget are kept only on your iPhone and are deleted when you delete the app.

Data we collect

None. Dandrey does not collect analytics, usage statistics, identifiers, or any personal data, and contains no third-party SDKs.

Subscriptions and payments

Dandrey Premium is an auto-renewable subscription sold through the App Store. Apple processes all payments, free trials, renewals and cancellations. We never see or store your payment details. The app only checks with the App Store whether your subscription is active. Apple processes this data as an independent controller under its own privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy

If you email us

If you contact support, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you (to provide the service you asked for, and our legitimate interest in supporting the app), keep it for up to 12 months after your request is resolved, and never share or sell it. You can ask us to access, correct or delete it at any time.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, Israel's Privacy Protection Law, or California law), you have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of personal data we hold about you. Because the app itself collects no personal data, in practice this covers only support emails. You can also complain to your local data protection authority, such as an EU supervisory authority or Israel's Privacy Protection Authority. To exercise these rights, email itay.bickson1@gmail.com.

Notifications

If you turn on cleanup reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. They are created on-device and are not sent through any server.

Children's privacy

Dandrey collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes
