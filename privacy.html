Dandrey — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-16

Dandrey ("the app") is designed to be private by default. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

The short version

  • Dandrey analyzes your photos and videos entirely on your device.
  • Your photos, videos, and any information derived from them never leave your iPhone and are never uploaded to us or any third party.
  • We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data.
  • There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking.

Photo library access

The app requests access to your photo library so it can:

  • scan photos and videos to find duplicates, near-duplicates, blurry, too-dark and overexposed shots, and large videos;
  • save new copies you ask for, such as a compressed version of a video or a still photo made from a Live Photo; and
  • move items you select to the system "Recently Deleted" album when you choose to clean up.

All analysis happens on your device, using Apple's on-device frameworks (such as Vision and Core ML) and a small image-recognition model built into the app. The app does not send your photos or anything about them off your device.

If you turn on the optional setting to analyze iCloud originals, the system may download those originals from your own iCloud account to your device for analysis. This happens through Apple's Photos framework, and the images stay on your device.

Deletion

When you choose to clean up, iOS asks you to confirm, and the selected items are moved to the system "Recently Deleted" album. They can be recovered there until you empty that album or iOS removes them. Dandrey cannot permanently delete your photos on its own.

Data we collect

None. Dandrey does not collect analytics, usage statistics, identifiers, or any personal data, and contains no third-party SDKs.

Notifications

If you turn on cleanup reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. They are created on-device and are not sent through any server.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes
