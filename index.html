Frequently asked questions

How much does Dandrey cost?

Scanning your library and seeing what Dandrey finds is free. Cleaning up, shrinking videos and converting Live Photos need Dandrey Premium: new subscribers get a 7-day free trial, then it's $9.99 per month in the US. Prices vary by country, include applicable taxes, and are shown before you subscribe. It renews automatically until you cancel.

How do I cancel my subscription or free trial?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Dandrey and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends to avoid being charged. You can also open Dandrey → Settings → Manage subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. If you live in the EU or UK, you can also cancel within 14 days of purchase there.

I'm already subscribed but the app asks me to pay.

Tap Restore Purchases at the bottom of the subscription screen, signed in with the Apple Account you subscribed with.

I deleted photos but my storage didn't go up. Why?

Deleted photos go to the Recently Deleted album first, so you can still get them back. Open Photos, go to Albums, then Recently Deleted, and tap Delete All to free the space.

Can I get back a photo I deleted?

Yes, for about 30 days. Open Photos, go to Albums, then Recently Deleted, select the photo and tap Recover.

Does Dandrey upload my photos?

No. All scanning happens on your iPhone. Dandrey never uploads or sends your photos anywhere. See the privacy policy.

Dandrey only sees some of my photos.

You probably gave it access to selected photos only. Open Settings, then Apps, then Dandrey, then Photos, and choose Full Access.

Does it delete anything on its own?

Never. You review everything, and iOS asks you to confirm before anything is moved to Recently Deleted.

Why does a scan take a while?

Dandrey analyzes every photo on your phone instead of in the cloud. Large libraries can take a few minutes the first time; later scans are faster.
